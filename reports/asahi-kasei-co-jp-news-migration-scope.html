02How the scope was built

The host has no sitemap.xml or robots.txt, and the CSR article folders return 403. The scope was therefore built by crawling the listing pages and following every link until no new URL turned up. Each URL was fetched, and its status, content type and title recorded.

08Risks & importer notes

Edge cases found during the crawl that the import scripts have to handle.

▲ high
Legacy markup (FY2006–2010)

554 pages use layout tables and <font> tags with no real title heading. The script has to build the heading and date itself.

MitigationSeparate legacy script; test JP and EN samples from every year in the Workbench.
● medium
Dead and redirected links

173 linked URLs are broken (mostly old IR PDFs) or redirect to asahi-kasei.com.

MitigationRewrite redirects to their target and unlink or flag broken ones. Report them in the import Excel.
● medium
Generic EN titles

EN FY2006–2010 articles all share a <title> such as “Fiscal 2006 / Press Releases”.

MitigationTake the page title from the article heading, never from <title>.
● medium
Wrong text encoding

/asahi/jp/news/2013/li130801.html is UTF-16 but declares UTF-8.

MitigationDetect the byte-order mark before parsing. Check the Japanese text after import.
○ low
CSR folders are locked

/csr/news/{yyyy}/ returns 403, so CSR articles can only be found through listing links.

MitigationThe URL list already covers them. Re-crawl if the listings change.
○ low
Duplicate aliases

Year listings also answer at /index.html; old CSR year indexes redirect to the CSR tab.

MitigationExcluded from the import. Add redirects in the EDS redirects sheet.
○ low
Links to the new domain

Internal links point to www.asahi-kasei.co.jp.

MitigationRewrite to the EDS paths (deck tip, slide 19).

Importer notes recorded in the scope summary

  • jp/news/2013/li130801.html is served as UTF-16 while declaring UTF-8: the importer must sniff the BOM before parsing.
  • EN FY2006–FY2010 article <title>s are generic ("Fiscal 2006 / Press Releases - Asahi Kasei"): take the page title from the article heading, not <title>.
  • Year roots also answer at /index.html (duplicate aliases, excluded). /csr/news/{yyyy}/index.html 301 to /news/{yyyy}/csr.html.
  • Listing tabs link to many old IR PDFs under /asahi/jp/ir/news/pdf/ and /asahi/en/ir/... that now 404 or 301 to asahi-kasei.com: rewrite or drop these links during import (see excluded.json).
